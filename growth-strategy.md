# Safqi growth strategy — Yemen first

> Arabic version: [`docs/ar/growth-strategy.md`](ar/growth-strategy.md). Keep both files in the same section order.
>
> Status: **strategy / playbook**, written 2026-11-13. Docs only. Product changes it recommends are listed in [§11](#11-follow-up-product-tasks) and are **not** built yet.
>
> Assumptions this plan is sized for: ad budget **under $100/month**, a sales team of **1–3 people**, and prices that **may change**.

---

## 1. Diagnosis — why merchants hesitate

Merchants sign up, look around, and leave. The reasons are not technical:

| Reason | What the merchant thinks | What it really is |
|---|---|---|
| The idea is new | «I sell fine on Instagram and WhatsApp, why do I need a store?» | They have never seen a Yemeni store work. Show them one (theirs). |
| Trust gap | «Who are you? Will you still be here in a year?» | No local proof yet: no testimonials, no known merchants. |
| No traffic promise | «Where will customers come from?» | They think a store is a market that brings buyers. It is a tool for the buyers they already have. |
| Price feels high | «9,500 a month is a lot.» | 9,500 Sana'a rial ≈ $18. That is real money for a micro-merchant, and we never showed what it pays back. |
| **Currency is ambiguous** | «Aden rial or Sana'a rial?» | The pricing page only says «ريال». A Sana'a merchant may read it as Aden rial pricing (≈ $4, "too cheap to be real") or the reverse, and simply stops replying. |

The last point is a **bug in our messaging, not in the market**. In the system, the platform currency `YER` is the Sana'a rial (1 USD = 530), while `YER_ADEN` is ≈ 2,400 per USD. The landing page never says which. Fix this first. It costs nothing and removes one of the four objections entirely.

## 2. Positioning

**One sentence:** Safqi doesn't replace your Instagram and WhatsApp. It turns your followers into organized orders.

- **One link in the bio.** Every product with its price, photos and sizes. No more «كم السعر؟» in DMs.
- **Orders arrive ready.** Name, phone, map location, and payment method (One Cash, Jaib, Kuraimi, bank transfer…). No voice notes asking «وين بيتكم؟».
- **The customer comes back.** Coupons, loyalty points, cashback and abandoned-cart reminders.
- **It's yours.** Your name, your design, your customers' data.

Rules for every message:
- **Multi-category always.** Show fashion, perfume, cosmetics, electronics, food and gifts side by side. Never a single-niche ad.
- **Plain words.** No "domain", "API", "dashboard", "SaaS". Say «رابط متجرك», «الطلبات», «لوحتك».
- **Talk about orders and time**, not features.

## 3. Objection playbook

Ready replies for WhatsApp / Instagram DMs. Send the Arabic as-is; the English is for the team.

### 3.1 «إيش أستفيد من الاشتراك؟» — What do I gain?

> أهلاً 👋 بدل ما تجاوب كل واحد «كم السعر؟ في مقاس؟ وين التوصيل؟»، الزبون يدخل رابط متجرك، يشوف الأسعار والمقاسات، ويطلب بنفسه، والطلب يوصلك جاهز بالاسم والرقم والموقع وطريقة الدفع.
> سوّينا لك متجر تجريبي من منتجاتك في انستقرام، شوفه هنا: [رابط المتجر التجريبي]
> جرّبه شهر كامل مجاناً، وإذا ما ارتحت ما تخسر شي.

*Meaning:* stop answering the same questions; orders arrive ready; here is **your** store, already built; try it free.

**Key move:** never explain Safqi in the abstract. Send them **their own store** (see [§7](#7-direct-sales-with-13-people)).

### 3.2 «من وين بيجوني الزبائن؟» — Where will customers come from?

Be honest. Overpromising here kills trust after the first month.

> سؤال مهم 👌 سافكي ما يجيب لك زبائن من الهواء، هو يخلي زبائنك الحاليين يشترون أسهل وأكثر.
> متابعينك في انستقرام، حالات الواتساب، القروبات، كلهم يدخلون من رابط واحد ويطلبون بدون ما ينتظروا ردك.
> وعندك أدوات تجيب زبون جديد وترجّع القديم: كوبونات، نقاط ولاء، كاش باك لما الزبون يشارك منتج لصاحبه، وتذكير للي ترك السلة.
> ونعطيك دليل «أول 100 طلب»: خطوات بسيطة تنشر فيها رابطك صح.

*Meaning:* Safqi converts the audience you already have; built-in tools bring new buyers (referral cashback) and bring old ones back; we give you a "first 100 orders" guide.

**The "first 100 orders" guide** (one page, sent to every new merchant):
1. Put the store link in the Instagram bio and as a story highlight «اطلب من هنا».
2. Every post caption ends with «الطلب من الرابط في البايو».
3. WhatsApp status: one product a day with the direct product link.
4. Send the link to your last 50 customers on WhatsApp with a 10% first-order coupon.
5. Turn on referral cashback so every buyer brings a friend.
6. Reply to every «كم السعر؟» comment with the product link, not the price.

### 3.3 «الأسعار غالية» — Prices are too high

Never argue. Reframe, then offer the entry plan.

> معك حق، كل ريال له حسابه 🙏
> بس احسبها كذا: لو ضاع عليك طلب واحد في الأسبوع لأنك ما رديت بسرعة، خسرت أكثر من الاشتراك.
> وعندنا باقة «البداية» بـ [السعر حسب منطقتك] في الشهر، يعني تقريباً حق كاسة شاي أو اثنتين في اليوم.
> وأول 100 متجر لهم خصم 40% مدى الحياة.

*Meaning:* one lost order a week costs more than the plan; the entry plan costs about one or two cups of tea a day; founding merchants get 40% off for life.

### 3.4 «بريال عدن ولا صنعاء؟» — Aden or Sana'a rial?

Always answer with the **merchant's own region and currency**. Never a bare «ريال».

**Sana'a-area merchant:**
> أسعارنا بالريال القديم (صنعاء) 👍
> باقة البداية 5,000 ريال صنعاء في الشهر، وتقدر تدفع من [محافظ/بنوك صنعاء].

**Aden-area merchant:**
> أسعارنا بالريال الجديد (عدن) 👍
> باقة البداية 22,000 ريال عدن في الشهر، وتقدر تدفع من ون كاش أو عدن كاش أو [بنوك عدن].

Why Sana'a merchants went silent: they couldn't tell whether 9,500 meant their rial, and they had no idea whether they could even pay from Sana'a. The answer must include **a local payment method** they recognize. Both regions are already configured in `config/payment_presets.php` (`region: sanaa | aden | both`).

## 4. Pricing proposal

A recommendation. Implementing it is a product task ([§11](#11-follow-up-product-tasks)).

### 4.1 Rules
1. **Always label the currency:** «ريال (صنعاء)» / «ريال (عدن)». A bare «ريال» is never allowed on the landing page, in ads, or in replies.
2. **One value, two price lists.** Price is anchored to a USD value and shown in each region's rial.
3. **Make the first step cheap.** Most Yemeni merchants are micro-businesses, so the decision to try must be almost free.

### 4.2 Proposed price list

Rates: 1 USD = 530 Sana'a rial, 1 USD = 2,400 Aden rial (the current `config/currency.php` values). **Check the live market rate before publishing** and round to friendly numbers.

| Plan | ≈ USD / month | Sana'a rial | Aden rial | For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter (new)** | ~$9 | 5,000 | 22,000 | First store, ~50 products, 1 user, no theme switching |
| Basic | ~$15 | 8,000 | 36,000 | Today's «أساسي» (was 9,500) |
| Pro | ~$25 | 13,000 | 60,000 | Today's «الاحترافية» (was 14,500) |
| Max | ~$33 | 17,500 | 80,000 | Today's «الشاملة» (was 18,500) |

### 4.3 Offers
- **30-day free trial** (today: 14 days), **or** free until the first 10 orders, whichever comes later. The merchant should feel the value (a real order) before paying.
- **Founding merchants:** the first 100 paying stores get **40% off for life**. Show a public counter («بقي 63 مقعد»). This creates urgency and gives us our first testimonials.
- **3-month option** next to monthly and yearly. Yemeni merchants avoid long commitments; yearly stays at ×10 (two months free).
- **First-month guarantee:** not happy, money back. It costs little and removes the fear of being cheated.

## 5. Instagram & Facebook ads — under $100/month

### 5.1 Setup (one time)
1. **Meta Business Manager** (business.facebook.com) owns the Facebook Page, the Instagram professional account and the ad account.
2. **WhatsApp Business** number linked to the Page, so ads can open a WhatsApp chat directly.
3. **Payment:** Yemeni cards are often rejected by Meta. Use a trusted ad-reseller/agency that bills in local money, or a card from abroad (a family member or partner). Agree on a written invoice for every top-up.
4. **Meta Pixel** on the landing page and the signup page, so we can retarget visitors and optimize for signups. Not built yet, see [§11](#11-follow-up-product-tasks).
5. **Custom audiences** to create once: video viewers 50%+ (30 days), Page/Instagram engagers (30 days), website visitors (when the Pixel is live).

### 5.2 Account structure (~$3/day ≈ $90/month)

| Campaign | Objective | Budget | Audience | Creative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. WhatsApp conversations** | Engagement → Messages (WhatsApp) | ~60% (≈ $1.8/day) | Yemen, 20–45, interests: online shopping, small business, Instagram business, fashion/perfume/cosmetics. **Two ad sets: Sana'a areas / Aden areas**, each with its own currency copy | The winning reel from campaign C |
| **B. Retargeting** | Messages or Traffic → signup | ~25% (≈ $0.75/day) | Video viewers 50%+ and page engagers, 30 days | Testimonials, founding-merchant offer, «سعرنا بريالك» |
| **C. Creative testing** | Engagement (ThruPlay) | ~15% (≈ $0.45/day, raise to $1/day during a test week) | Broad Yemen | 3 new reels at a time, 5–7 days |

Notes:
- **Click-to-WhatsApp beats website clicks** in Yemen. People trust a conversation, and our 1–3 sales people close in chat.
- Use **location targeting by governorate** for the Sana'a / Aden split, and **never mix the two currencies in one ad**.
- Leave the targeting broad inside each region. Small budgets learn faster with fewer, larger ad sets.
- **Boost what already works.** If an organic reel gets above-average reach, put $5 behind it rather than creating a cold ad.

### 5.3 Weekly routine (30 minutes, every Sunday)
1. Read: cost per WhatsApp conversation, conversations → signups, signups → first order, CTR per ad.
2. Turn off any ad with **CTR under 1%** or a cost per conversation more than 2× the average.
3. Move the best ad from C into A.
4. Upload the next 3 test reels to C.
5. Write the numbers in the KPI sheet ([§10](#10-90-day-roadmap--metrics)).

### 5.4 Starting targets (guesses to recalibrate after month 1)
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Cost per WhatsApp conversation | < $0.30 |
| Conversation → signup | ≥ 15% |
| Cost per signup | < $2 |
| Trial → paid | ≥ 20% |

At $90/month that is ≈ 300 conversations → ≈ 45 signups → ≈ 9 paying stores/month from ads alone, before direct sales and referrals.

## 7. Direct sales with 1–3 people

Ads start conversations; people close them. With a small team the trick is to **show, not tell**.

### 7.1 The demo-store pipeline (main engine)
Per sales person, per day:
1. **Shortlist 20 Instagram shops** in Yemen: active, posting products with photos, selling through DMs. Mix categories.
2. **Build 5 demo stores** from their Instagram with the `instagram-store-builder` skill (logo, products from their posts, a fitting theme).
3. **DM the merchant** with their store link:
   > السلام عليكم 🌷 أعجبتنا منتجاتكم، فسوّينا لكم متجر إلكتروني جاهز باسمكم ومنتجاتكم، شوفوه: [الرابط]
   > الزبون يدخل، يختار، ويطلب مباشرة. لو حبيتوه نسلّمه لكم مجاناً شهر كامل.
4. **Follow up** on WhatsApp on day 1, day 3 and day 7. Three touches, then stop.

That is ~25 demo stores a week per person and ~100 in month 1 with 1 person.

### 7.2 Field visits
- Markets with many small shops: Sana'a (Hadda, Al-Tahrir, Shumaila), Aden (Crater, Al-Mansoura, Sheikh Othman), Taiz, Mukalla.
- Bring a phone with 3 demo stores in different categories, set up the store on the spot, and add the first 10 products together.

### 7.3 Done-for-you onboarding
Every new merchant gets: the first 10 products uploaded by us, payment methods set up for their region, and the store link put in their Instagram bio. Removing the "I don't have time to set it up" excuse is worth more than any feature.

### 7.4 CRM sheet (Google Sheets is enough)
Columns: shop, category, city/region (Sana'a/Aden), Instagram, WhatsApp, demo-store link, owner (sales person), stage, last contact, next step.
Stages: **lead → demo built → contacted → replied → trial → first order → paid → referred someone**.

## 8. Referral, ambassadors & partnerships

### 8.1 Merchant referral
The referral program already exists (`config/referral.php`: a referred tenant gets a 30-day trial). Add the merchant reward: **one free month for every referred store that pays**. Merchants know other merchants; this is our cheapest channel.

### 8.2 Ambassadors
One commission-based ambassador per governorate (students, freelancers, ad designers who already work with shops). Commission: 30–50% of the first paid month, or a fixed amount per paying store. Give them a demo-store kit and the replies in §3.

### 8.3 Partnerships
| Partner | What they get | What we get |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery companies (HiExpress, ShipCircle, local couriers) | Orders from our stores | They recommend Safqi to their merchants |
| Wallets (One Cash, Jaib, Kuraimi, Aden Cash…) | Payment volume | Co-marketing, a «متاجر تقبل [المحفظة]» listing |
| Training centers, women's business groups | A practical course «افتح متجرك» | Groups of motivated merchants at once |
| Micro-influencers (5k–50k followers) | A free store for their own products | Honest «فتحت متجري» content |

## 9. Activation — the trial is where we win or lose

**Goal: the first order within 7 days.** A merchant who receives one real order almost always pays.

| Day | Action (WhatsApp from the sales person, plus a personal call within 24h) |
|---|---|
| 0 | Welcome call. Set up payment methods for their region, upload the first 10 products together |
| 1 | Send the "first 100 orders" guide (§3.2). Ask them to put the link in the bio |
| 2 | Check: link in bio? If not, help them do it |
| 3 | Suggest a first-order coupon to their last 50 customers |
| 5 | «وصلك أول طلب؟» If not, review the store together and fix photos/prices |
| 7 | Celebrate the first order (ask permission to share it in our stories) |
| 25 | Remind them the trial ends, with the founding-merchant offer |

## 10. 90-day roadmap & metrics

### Month 1 — Fix the basics
- Region labels on every price (landing page, ads, replies). Publish the new price list and the founding-merchant offer.
- 30-day trial.
- Ads account set up, Pixel installed, campaigns A/B/C running.
- 100 demo stores built and sent.
- Make the «سعرنا بريالك» and «من وين الزبائن» videos.

### Month 2 — Proof
- Collect the first 5 testimonials (video + screenshot of orders).
- Recruit 2–3 ambassadors.
- Scale the winning creatives (move budget from C to A/B).
- Start the merchant referral reward.

### Month 3 — Scale
- Sign 2 partnerships (one delivery company, one wallet).
- Push yearly/3-month plans to founding merchants.
- Run a training session in one city.

### KPI sheet (update weekly)
| Metric | Definition | Month-1 target |
|---|---|---|
| Signups | New stores created | 60 |
| Activation | Stores with ≥ 1 real order within 14 days | 40% of signups |
| Trial → paid | Paid at trial end | 20% |
| CAC | (Ads + commissions) ÷ new paying stores | < $10 |
| Churn | Paying stores that cancel in the month | < 10% |
| Source | Ads / demo store / referral / field / partner | Tracked per store |
